I tried your code and it works fine even if I remove the MAX_DIRECTIONS limit. About the final value, 25929 is not correct (but close to the correct one). The final update to the value is not performed by the process of rank 0, but by process of rank 'pos'. So, before printing the final value, you had to broadcast the value to all processes. What you are getting (25929) is the value last modified by rank 0
